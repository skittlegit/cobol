## Objective

Audit the frozen artifacts before interpreting scores, publish the stratified
benchmark-first findings and limitations, consume the anti-gaming handoff, and
close M5 without upgrading an inconclusive comparison into a claim.

## Required analysis

1. Validate manifests, hashes, paired row identity, infrastructure counts,
   annotation evidence, and reuse decisions before reading headline metrics.
2. State the M4 NO_GO unchanged and distinguish benchmark contribution from
   detector performance.
3. Report every baseline, the attacker-with-bases floor, the artifact-only
   leakage gate, local/interprocedural results, D1-D7 results, coverage,
   faithfulness tiers, calibration, and T6 uncertainty.
4. Carry the T0.6 benchmark-first related-work framing forward without claiming
   that failure alone establishes novelty or practical utility.
5. Record threats to validity: synthetic/real mix, 51 reviewed real-curated
   candidates but only 43 frozen rows, CI-fragile cells, regulation/domain
   scope, source reconstruction, the historical review population and its
   declared model/human provenance, model identity, and temporal-pair
   dependence. Never relabel AI review as human review.
6. Clear the T2.2 Track C flag only when its six-feature result and paired floor
   appear in the final report.

## M5 close rule

M5 closes when T5.1-T5.5 are all done, benchmark v1 and the datasheet are
immutable, the required report is evaluable, and the analysis is committed.
Closing M5 records the measured result; it does not require the agent to pass
the M4 bars and does not activate post-release M8.
