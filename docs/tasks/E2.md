# E2 — Second evaluation with corrected temporal programs

**State:** done — GO

## Why

E1 (NO_GO) failed only the temporal gate, 15/22. Reading the failed pairs
showed that several temporal programs written for B2 test the old threshold
but omit part of the clause, so their conformant (old-side) labels were
wrong. E2 corrects the programs and re-runs the temporal pairs. It is a
separate, later evaluation; E1's NO_GO stands as the result of the first
official run (commit 08e2c6f0).

## Protocol (fixed before any E2 result)

- Detector unchanged: the frozen version from E1 (source unchanged since
  ea5db367; `codex.method_hash()` prefix 5fc235450c18f761), gpt-6-luna at
  effort `max`. Gates unchanged.
- Every one of the 22 temporal programs was reviewed against every leg of its
  clause: company (shares, capital, or profits above the limit, or control
  through other means), trust (author, trustee, controller, and beneficiaries
  at or above the limit), partnership (capital or profits above the limit,
  plus the control route of the 2025 version). Seven were incomplete and were
  rewritten: TPCO02 (management or policy control), TPCO04 (capital and
  profits), TPCO06 (capital), TPCO08 (control route), TPTR07 and TPTR08
  (author, trustee, controller), TPPA02 (control). The other fifteen are
  unchanged. All 22 compile and pass 51 behaviour checks under GnuCOBOL 3.2.0
  (old threshold applied; every added route works).
- All 44 temporal rows get new ids and are re-run, including the unchanged
  pairs. Up to three passes; further re-runs only for infrastructure failures,
  counted and reported.
- The test split and baseline results are E1's, unchanged. `report --split
  test` recomputes the decision with the new temporal results.
- The test split's known weakness (D6 recall 4/22) is not addressed: changing
  the detector after seeing test results would be tuning on test data.

## Result (2026-10-08)

**GO.** Temporal paired accuracy 19/22 = 0.864 (E1: 15/22). Every other gate
is E1's test-split result, unchanged: T1 F1 0.882, balanced accuracy 0.842,
answer rate 1.000, answered accuracy 0.848, cross-program F1 advantage over
rag_reranker +0.165 (CI 0.089..0.258, p = 0.0001, n = 60), zero unverified
findings.

Runs: three passes left 2 rows failed on infrastructure (drift_190066 and
drift_190068, shell syntax in a `check_finding` call); both succeeded on a
fourth pass, as the protocol allows.

Remaining failures, all on the old (conformant) side: pair-02 (TPCO02) and
pair-07 (TPCO07) called D2 because the program's "policy control" flag was
not read as covering control of management; pair-04 (TPCO04) called D3,
claiming that `COMPUTE ... ROUNDED` can round a share just above 25 percent
down to 25.00, which is a real edge of that program's arithmetic.

Caveat for readers: E2 follows an inspection of E1's failures. The detector
and gates did not change, but the corrected programs were written after
seeing which pairs failed, so E2 is a post-hoc re-evaluation of a repaired
benchmark, not a first look.
