## Objective

Publish the detector-led end-to-end migration result and the separately labeled
oracle-assisted patcher upper bound, including all failures and capability
limits.

- roster identity and eligible/evaluated denominators;
- patch, abstention, apply, parse, compile, intended-test, and regression rates;
- results by D1-D6 and local/interprocedural stratum;
- affected-line precision and unrelated-change counts;
- batch versus CICS validation capability;
- Luna/max tokens, turns, latency, and interruption/resume evidence;
- representative successes and failures; and
- exact relationship to the configuration-3 detector decision.

## Release rule

An oracle-assisted success does not rescue a detector `NO_GO`. An end-to-end
migration claim requires the T8.1 detector utility gate plus valid detector-led
patch evidence. A disappointing or unevaluable result is retained unchanged.

T6.4 closes when the machine-readable report, narrative, manifests, and raw
evidence reconcile and T5.5 can consume the final detector and migration
decisions.
