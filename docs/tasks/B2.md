# B2 — Fresh temporal pairs

**State:** done

## Approach

Build at least 20 new pairs. Each pair is identical code judged against two
regulation versions with opposite verdicts (one D7 side, one drift side), drawn
from the 2022/2024/2025 clause history. Claude reviews every pair (a different
model family from the detector); there is no human review pass. Replace `data/benchmark/temporal/` with the new pairs; the current 20
pairs join dev.

## Done when

`pairs.json` lists at least 20 reviewed pairs, every pair materializes, and
both sides of each pair share byte-identical source.

## Result (2026-10-07)

22 pairs, 44 rows, in `data/benchmark/temporal/` (`programs/`, `rows.jsonl`,
`pairs.json`). Each program was written by Claude, keeps the threshold of the
2016 KYC Master Direction as consolidated 2018-07-12, and was compiled and
behaviour-checked under GnuCOBOL 3.2.0: a value between the new and old
limits is not identified, a value past the old limit is.

| Authority target | Old (2018-07-12) | New (2025-11-28) | Pairs | Cross-program |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Company controlling ownership, 3(a)(iv)(a) → 5(iv)(a) | > 25% | > 10% | 10 | 3 |
| Trust beneficiaries, 3(a)(iv)(d) → 5(iv)(d) | >= 15% | >= 10% | 8 | 2 |
| Partnership, 3(a)(iv)(b) → 5(iv)(b) | > 15% | > 10% | 4 | 2 |

Shapes vary: inline literals, named constants, 88-level ranges, computed
percentages, table scans, EVALUATE, shared copybooks, called modules, and a
limit passed by the caller. The company and trust changes come from the
2023-04-28 circular (footnotes 7 and 9 of the final 2016 consolidation); the
partnership change from 2023-10-17 (footnote 8).

Checks: every pair materializes to byte-identical source on both sides with
opposite verdicts (D7 / D1). The 20 previous pairs (40 rows) were moved into
dev (22 rows were added; 18 were already there) and their programs into
`seed/programs/`. The stale temporal results file was removed.

Not used: the CKYCR seven-day update deadline (the pinned pre-2023
consolidation predates the paragraph, so its old wording cannot be cited
from a pinned source) and the card directions (no numeric obligation changed
between the 2022 Master Direction and the 2025 Directions).
