optimizing it or claiming deployment utility.

- end-to-end and per-tool latency;
- tokens, turns, tool calls, retries, and successful observations;
- repeated parsing, retrieval, slicing, and verification work;
- cache reads/writes where the provider exposes them;
- allocation by drift class and local/interprocedural stratum;
- coverage gained per additional tool call versus the historical-policy
  Luna/max control; and

Lower resource use is not an improvement unless the final output still passes
the T8.1 quality and evidence gates. No dollar cost is inferred from in-product
collaboration-subagent runs without a metered billing record. Optional legacy
native/WSL evidence, if ever produced, remains separately labeled.

## Completion

manifests and missing telemetry is reported rather than estimated.
