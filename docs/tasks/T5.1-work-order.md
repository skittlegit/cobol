## Objective

Freeze a reviewer-auditable annotation protocol before adding or independently
relabeling benchmark rows. The protocol must make two qualified annotators
produce the frozen `DriftInstance` semantics from the same regulation and code
evidence without access to mutation-generation metadata.

This phase writes and gates `ANNOTATION.md`. It does not regenerate, scale, or
freeze the benchmark. Those later T5.1 phases begin only after the guidelines
are committed and the sampling/adjudication plan is predeclared.

## Scale-up and independent-pass amendment — ratified 2026-07-27

The T2.7 expansion already supplies the minimum Phase-5 scale: 51 real-curated
rows, including 20 intact verdict-flipping T6 pairs. T5.1 therefore does not
generate additional rows. It independently annotates all 51 rows.

Both annotators receive the exact same
`data/benchmark/annotation/real_curated_blinded.jsonl` pack. The pack contains
the pinned clause and source bundle but omits drift class, target path, labels,
gold rationale, provenance, mutation metadata, prior judgments, and existing
gold. Each pass must use a distinct named annotator, cover all 51 candidate
IDs, and be timestamped before either pass is disclosed to the other.

The pre-adjudication report publishes sample size, raw inclusion and class
agreement with bootstrap 95% intervals, Cohen's kappa, Krippendorff's nominal
alpha, and structured disagreement counts. No minimum agreement statistic
silently changes a label: all disagreements are adjudicated against primary
evidence and retained in an immutable change log. T5.1 completes only when:

1. both independent 51-row passes validate;
2. the agreement report is generated from those locked passes;
3. every disagreement is adjudicated or explicitly excluded;
4. the resulting real-curated rows round-trip `DriftInstance`; and
5. no unresolved `needs_adjudication` record enters the frozen benchmark.

`src/cobol_archaeologist/benchmark/annotation.py` owns the blinded projection,
typed pass records, and deterministic agreement calculation. A model, agent,
or the benchmark generator may prepare evidence but may not be represented as
a qualified human annotator.

## Locked requirements

1. Define the annotation unit, evidence precedence, admissible evidence, and
   fail-closed handling for unresolved regulatory or source questions.
2. Give operational inclusion/exclusion rules for D1–D7. D7 requires positive
   matching evidence and is never the default when no defect is found.
3. Bind every schema field annotators set: clause identity and date,
   `current_value`, `code_locus.loci`, `slice_vars`, `is_interprocedural`,
   `target_path`, multi-level labels, `gold_rationale`, and `provenance`.
4. Preserve the D2 insertion-point convention and the one-way
   interprocedurality rule from CONTRACT v1.4.
5. Preserve T6 pairing by byte-identical `code_locus`; do not invent a
   `pair_id`. Carry T2.1's P1 old-side `defensible-ambiguous` stance explicitly.
6. Separate annotator-visible evidence from gold-only construction metadata.
   Mutation operator names, mutation diffs, generator seeds, judge verdicts,
   and existing labels are hidden during independent annotation.
7. Require two independent passes before discussion, structured disagreement
   reasons, an adjudicator who sees both rationales, and an immutable change
   log. Report raw agreement, Cohen's κ where categories permit it, and
   Krippendorff's α for missing/multi-valued decisions; never report agreement
   only after adjudication.
8. Prohibit edit-artifact cues, history, mtimes, formatting discontinuities,
   and comment freshness as label evidence. Preserve the artifact-only
   `literal_roundness` gate and the attacker-with-bases probe as evaluation
   controls, not annotation shortcuts.
