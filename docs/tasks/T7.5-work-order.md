  decisions and denominators.
- Related-work citations are primary where available, current at submission,
  and support the exact claim made.
- Anonymous artifacts contain no credentials, usernames, local absolute paths,
  chat history, or non-release provider logs.
- The final paper builds from a clean checkout; references, figures, tables,
  and appendix links resolve.
- T5.5, T7.2, and T7.3 are done before the final submission package is marked
  complete.
